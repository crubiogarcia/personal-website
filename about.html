About

I started in architecture, but my work has increasingly taken me towards computation, programming, and artificial intelligence. I currently work in Applied Research & Development (ARD) at Foster + Partners, developing computational methods and software tools for the built environment.

I am particularly interested in using algorithms, computational geometry, machine learning, and data-driven systems to solve complex spatial problems across buildings and cities, with applications in complex geometry pipelines, resource efficiency, and environmental performance.
