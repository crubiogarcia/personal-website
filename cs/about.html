About

I trained as an architect, but over time I became more interested in the computational side of the work: programming, geometry, algorithms and AI. That gradually became the main focus of what I do.

I now work in Applied Research & Development at Foster + Partners, where I develop software tools and computational methods for design and engineering problems. My work involves geometry, automation, data and machine learning.

What I’m most interested in is how complex spatial problems can be translated into computational ones: how complex geometry can be represented, how rules and constraints can become algorithms, and how intelligent systems can work with spatial and physical information.
