ReDesignMate — Adaptive Reuse Framework

SOFTWARE · STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS · ADAPTIVE REUSE

A computational design tool for exploring different ways to adapt and reuse existing buildings, comparing renovation options through structural and carbon analysis

grasshopper · rhino · rhino compute · c# · javascript · structural analysis · carbon analysis

Built at the AEC Hackathon, London · 2025, with Jeroen Janssen, Rey Duong and Iliana Papadopoulous

Overview

ReDesignMate investigates how computational design can support the reuse and transformation of existing buildings rather than their replacement.

The tool generates alternative renovation scenarios from configurable design parameters, evaluates their structural implications, and compares façade systems through performance and carbon-footprint analysis.

A web interface connects directly to parametric Grasshopper models through Rhino Compute, allowing design alternatives to be generated and assessed interactively.

The project explores how environmental impact can become an active design parameter during early-stage renovation — helping compare interventions not only geometrically, but through their structural and carbon consequences.

Process
01 — Generate

Configurable parameters produce multiple renovation and extension scenarios from the existing building.

02 — Evaluate

Alternatives are tested against structural constraints and compared through façade, material and embodied-carbon performance.

03 — Reuse

The project explores how computational design can support adaptive reuse by treating existing buildings as resources to be transformed rather than replaced.
